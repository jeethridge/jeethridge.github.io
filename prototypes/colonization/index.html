what the model computes
  • uptake Hyphae absorb nutrient from the substrate with Michaelis–Menten kinetics.
  • translocation Absorbed nutrient diffuses through the network from fed regions to starved ones.
  • extension Each tip grows at a rate that saturates with the nutrient behind it. A tip that starves for 2 h stops.
  • direction A persistent random walk, bent away from dense mycelium and weakly up the nutrient gradient.
  • branching Lateral branches leave about 0.2 mm behind the tip, more often when the tip is well fed.
  • fusion A tip that touches another hypha can fuse with it, which turns the tree into a network.
